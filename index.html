My Story — One Person, One Vision

PickZone was born from a simple yet powerful idea: to bring the joy of quality shopping to every corner of Sri Lanka. What started as a personal dream has grown into a trusted online shopping platform serving thousands of customers.

Behind PickZone is just one passionate individual who believes that everyone deserves access to great products at fair prices. Every order is personally handled with care and attention.

Today, PickZone proudly serves customers across all 25 districts of Sri Lanka, delivering not just products, but happiness right to their doorsteps.
